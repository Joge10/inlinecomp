Quick Reference — for yourself, your son or daughter, and supporters
🔍
Open the public app: inlineresults.devriesen.com/public/
🆕 New in June 2026: Faster page loads and more stable results refresh (behind-the-scenes optimisation). No functional changes for the public.

1Choose your race

At the top of the page is a dropdown with all races. By default you see today only.

  • Earlier: past races (after they finished)
  • Today: live (default on)
  • Later: upcoming

Buttons can be toggled independently. Picking a race activates the schedule + starts auto-refresh.

Races in preparation appear as "(coming soon)" — not clickable until the organisation releases them.

2Find yourself, your son or daughter

One input field accepts three kinds of search — just type and click Search:

  • Bib number (e.g. 432) — fastest way
  • Licence number (e.g. 78901)
  • Surname (e.g. Jansen) — if multiple matches, a selection popup appears

Want to follow more than one person (e.g. yourself + your child, or brother + sister)? After the first search, tabs appear at the top; click + to add someone, or the cross to remove someone.

Practical tips

  • 👥 Multiple people: after the first search, tabs appear at the top; click + to add yourself, your son or daughter, the cross to remove someone.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Siblings in one go: searching by a surname with multiple matches opens a selection popup. Tick several people at once and click OK — every ticked person is added as a tab immediately.
  • 💾 Add once, remembered forever: the people you follow are stored locally in your browser by licence number. At the next race they reappear automatically — no need to search again.
  • 🔄 Auto-refresh: schedule & results refresh on their own. The time stamp top-right shows the last update.
  • ↓ Pull to refresh: pull the screen down for an immediate refresh (on phone).
  • 📡 No connection? A red banner appears at the top; data refreshes automatically as soon as the network returns.
  • 📢 Announcements: important messages from the organisation appear as a pop-up (e.g. "Schedule running 15 min late").
  • 🛠 Install as app: on your phone → "Add to home screen" → own icon without browser bar, faster start.
  • 🔗 Race detail: in the Schedule tab, click a race to open the full starting list in an overlay.

Troubleshooting — short

SymptomWhat to do
"I can't find myself / my son / my daughter" Check that you picked the right race. Try searching by bib number instead of name (surname must match exactly). For late registrations via the organisation, it can take ~1 minute before the entry becomes visible.
Heats do not appear The starting list only appears once the race director has drawn the heats. Until then you only see the Schedule tab with a "🚩 not yet final" indicator.
Times no longer update Pull to refresh or wait for the auto-refresh. If you see the red banner: as soon as the connection returns, the app refreshes by itself.
Your results tab is empty Official results only appear once the race director confirms them. Times from the heat table are already visible during the race.
