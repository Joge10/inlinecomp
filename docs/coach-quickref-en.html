InlineComp Coach

Quick Reference — for coaches following multiple skaters at once
👥
Open the coach app: inlineresults.devriesen.com/coach/
🆕 New in June 2026: Faster page loads (significant behind-the-scenes optimisation of the race API). No functional changes for coaches.

1Choose your race

At the top of the page is a dropdown with all races. By default you see today only.

  • Earlier: past races (after they finished)
  • Today: live (default on)
  • Later: upcoming

Buttons can be toggled independently. Picking a race activates the schedule + starts auto-refresh.

Races in preparation appear as "(coming soon)" — not clickable until the organisation releases them.

2Add skaters

Fill in one or more fields and click + Add:

  • Club: button "— pick club(s) —" → panel with checkboxes → tick multiple clubs at once → Done. Selected clubs appear as chips below the button.
  • Sponsor: same widget, select multiple sponsors at once possible aswell.
  • Bib number: one skater per number.

All three can be combined in a single click on + Add. Added skaters appear as chips at the top; click a chip = remove from your list.

Practical tips

  • 💾 Remembered per race: your skater list is stored locally in your browser per race — it comes back when you reopen the page later.
  • 🔄 Auto-refresh: schedule & penalties refresh on their own. The time stamp bottom-right shows the last update.
  • ↓ Pull to refresh: pull the schedule down for an immediate refresh (on phone).
  • 📡 No connection? A red banner appears at the top; data refreshes automatically as soon as the network returns.
  • 📢 Announcements: important messages from the organisation appear as a pop-up (e.g. "Schedule running 15 min late").
  • 🛠 Install as app: on your phone → "Add to home screen" → own icon without browser bar, faster start.

Troubleshooting — short

SymptomWhat to do
My skater is not in the list Check that you picked the right race. For late registrations via the organisation: the row gets status Conf. by org. — can take ~1 minute to appear.
Heats do not appear The starting list is only published once the race director has drawn the heats. Until then you see "⏳ not final".
Times no longer update Pull to refresh or wait for the auto-refresh. If you see the red banner: as soon as the connection returns, the app refreshes by itself.
Skater list looks different The skater list is stored per race. Switching between races starts with an empty list — your previous selection reappears when you go back.
